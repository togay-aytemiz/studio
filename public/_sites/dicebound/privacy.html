Privacy Policy

How Dicebound handles game data, website visits and messages you send us.

This policy covers Dicebound and dicebound.agens.studio, published by Agens Studio. The game information below reflects the current version.

Your game, on your device

Dicebound does not require an account. Game progress, inventory, rewards, activity timers and preferences are stored on your device so you can continue playing. Local transaction records help recover interrupted saves and prevent the same reward from being granted twice. These records are not advertising identifiers.

Data and permissions

The current game has no advertising, account service or developer-operated gameplay analytics. We do not sell your information or upload your game progress to our own servers. Dicebound does not request access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Optional notifications

If you choose to enable alerts, your device can remind you when Gathering, Crafting or Auto Hunt finishes. These reminders are scheduled locally; they do not require a remote push-notification account. You can decline alerts and continue playing, or change notification permission in your device settings.

Device services

The Unity runtime uses local storage and system information needed to run the game, read and write saves, and apply preferences. Your operating system or store may separately process purchase, diagnostic or backup information under its own policies and your settings. A device backup may include your saved game; Dicebound does not provide a separate cloud-save account.

Keeping and removing data

Local saves and preferences remain on your device while they are needed to continue your game. On iPhone, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app can retain it. Copies in device backups are managed through your device or backup provider. Removing a save or backup may permanently remove your progress, and we cannot restore a local save from a game server.

This website

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies. Fonts and images are served from this site. When you visit, the hosting service receives technical information such as your IP address, requested page and browser information to deliver the site. Hosting and security logs may be processed by the hosting provider to operate and protect the service.

Version checks

A release with update checks enabled requests a small version-policy file from our website. The hosting service receives normal connection information, including the IP address and requested URL. The game does not include your save, inventory, email address or advertising identifier in that request. A valid update requirement can be stored on your device for later checks.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@agens.studio. If you email us, we use the contact details and information you choose to provide to respond to your request. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence at the same address. We keep it only as long as needed to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Any new permission will be requested when the corresponding feature needs it.
