What to include

  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • The game version, if available.
  • What you were doing and what happened.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Please hide personal information.

Never send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information.

Keep your progress safe

Please do not delete or reinstall the game as a first troubleshooting step. Progress is stored on your device, and deleting the app may remove it. Contact us before removing game data.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Dicebound stores progress and preferences on your device and does not require an account.

Can I move my save to another device?

Dicebound does not currently provide its own cloud-save or account-transfer service. Device backups are handled by your operating system and backup provider.

How do I turn off notifications?

Open your device’s notification settings and change the permission for Dicebound. The game’s activity reminders are scheduled locally.

What should I do if the game asks for an update?

Open the update link shown in the game. If the store does not offer an update, email us with your installed version and store country. Keep your game installed while we investigate.
